ABOUT THE QUIZ

The same words,
different clocks.

“A while,” “soon,” and “just a minute” sound specific until you try to put them on a clock. This free, crowd-sourced time quiz explores how people interpret those everyday phrases. Pick a question, give your estimate in seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, months, or years, and compare it with the community.

Everyday questions, answered your way

There are more than 100 prompts about ordinary moments: how long a quick shower takes, what counts as a short walk, when a late friend is really late, or how long a long weekend feels. Browse by topic, search for a phrase, or sort to find questions that need an answer.

How the crowd’s answers are grouped

Different units can describe the same duration, so answers are converted to a common scale before results are grouped. For this quiz, a month is treated as 30 days and a year as 365 days. Results show the number and share of responses for each estimate.

Answers are stored as anonymous totals, not tied to an account. A small reminder in your browser keeps track of questions you have answered on that device.
